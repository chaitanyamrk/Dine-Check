Heart Cup Coffee Restaurant and Bar

Road No. 45, Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad

Enforcement record — no hygiene score.
Action recorded — reported 13 December 2024 by TG SAFE.
This is not a scored audit and is not ranked against places that have one.

This record is from 13 December 2024. It describes conditions on that day and may not reflect the place today.

Violations recorded

  • Water analysis reports, Pest Control records and Fostac certificates of employees were not provided
  • Refrigerators are not cleaned properly
  • Live cockroach infestation was observed in the kitchen
  • Kissan Tomato Paste (6kg), Vanaspathi (10kg), Oregano (1kg) and Peri Peri Marinade (8kg) were found to be expired and hence discarded
  • Flooring was found to be patchy and littering of food waste at some places
  • Kitchen floor is not equipped with drains to remove food waste
  • Ceiling of the kitchen was found to be covered with soot
  • Walls were found to be oily and tiles were found to be broken at some places
  • Exhaust was found to be greasy and unclean
  • Vegetables like tomatoes and potatoes were found in spoilt condition and discarded
  • Raw meat like chicken, mutton etc were found to be dumped in the refrigerator in unhygienic manner which may cause contamination

Source: TG SAFE, 13 December 2024
