Central Kitchen of Sri Chaitanya educational institutes

Madhapur, Hyderabad

Enforcement record — no hygiene score.
Action recorded — reported 24 January 2025 by TG SAFE.
This is not a scored audit and is not ranked against places that have one.

This record is from 24 January 2025. It describes conditions on that day and may not reflect the place today.

Violations recorded

  • Water analysis reports, Pest Control records and Fostac certificates of employees were not provided
  • Refrigerators were not cleaned properly and were found to be unhygienic
  • Flooring was found to be patchy and wet with littering of food waste at some places
  • Drains in the kitchen floor are found to be broken at places and are also not fitted to meshes to prevent entry of insects
  • Walls were found to be patchy at places with broken tiles and it was also found to be greasy
  • Exhaust was found to be greasy and unclean
  • Dustbins were found in open condition
  • Doors are not close fitted to prevent entry of pests. The grills between the walls and the ceiling were not fitted with meshes to prevent the entry of insects
  • Packed Nut Meg was found to be unlabeled
  • Most of the food articles were found to be stored on ground instead of pallets. Food and non food articles were stored together
  • Kaju Nuka (125 Kg) expired on 17.01.2025 were seized
  • Walls of the storage were found to be patchy at places and were also found to be unclean
  • Live cockroach infestation and rodent feces were observed at several places and no rodent traps were found in the storage
  • Food handlers were found to be without aprons, gloves etc
  • The unit has (12) transport vehicles but the FSSAI license of the FBO does not include transport category and there is also no separate FSSAI license for transport vehicles

Source: TG SAFE, 24 January 2025
