Poshnosh Lounge & Bar

Road No 36, Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad

Enforcement record — no hygiene score.
Action recorded — reported 1 February 2025 by TG SAFE.
This is not a scored audit and is not ranked against places that have one.

This record is from 1 February 2025. It describes conditions on that day and may not reflect the place today.

Violations recorded

  • Found to be operating with expired license (20.12.2024)
  • Water analysis reports, Pest Control records and Fostac certificates and medical records of employees were not provided
  • Refrigerators were not defrosted and cleaned properly
  • Kitchen floor is not equipped with drains to remove food waste
  • Kitchen had an opening to the outside without window and insect proof screen
  • Veg and non-veg food, food and non-food items were found to be stored together. Food articles were found to be stored on ground
  • Expired and misbranded Sichuan Pepper found and seized – product of Bangkok but importer details not available
  • Packed Citric Acid (1kg) meant only for industrial use found in the storage
  • Expired food items including Soy Sauce (1L - Sep 2024), Papad packets (18), Rasam powder (1kg - Jul 2024), Onion powder (Oct 2024), Kajun mix (Dec 2024), Mushroom (800g), Packed Methi (2kg) were found and discarded
  • Loose Split Cashew was found to be infested with insects

Source: TG SAFE, 1 February 2025
