Taaza Kitchen

Ameerpet, Hyderabad

Enforcement record — no hygiene score.
Action recorded — reported 6 March 2025 by TG SAFE.
This is not a scored audit and is not ranked against places that have one.

This record is from 6 March 2025. It describes conditions on that day and may not reflect the place today.

Violations recorded

  • Water Analysis Report and Fostac Certificates of supervisor not available
  • Refrigerators were not maintained properly. Dustbins were found kept in open condition
  • Food articles in the refrigerator were found to be uncovered and unlabelled
  • Semi-prepared and raw materials, food and non-food items were stored together. Few vegetables (tomatoes) found to be spoiled
  • Rusty iron knives were found being used for cutting of vegetables
  • Storage was found to be small and congested. Several food articles were found to be stored on ground instead of pallets
  • Rice flour stored in a steel container was found to be infested with insects and hence discarded
  • Temperature records for the cold room were not maintained
  • Some of the food handlers were found without hairnets, gloves etc

Source: TG SAFE, 6 March 2025
