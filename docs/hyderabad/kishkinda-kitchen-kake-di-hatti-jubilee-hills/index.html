Kishkinda Kitchen (Kake di hatti)

Road No. 36, Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad

Enforcement record — no hygiene score.
Action recorded — reported 1 February 2025 by TG SAFE.
This is not a scored audit and is not ranked against places that have one.

This record is from 1 February 2025. It describes conditions on that day and may not reflect the place today.

Violations recorded

  • Water analysis reports, Fostac certificates and medical records of employees were not provided
  • Refrigerators are found to be littered with food waste and not cleaned properly
  • Drains in the kitchen were found to be clogged with food waste
  • Semi prepared food articles and raw materials, food and non-food items were found to be stored together
  • Vegetables like lemons, potatoes etc were found in spoiled condition
  • Live cockroach infestation observed in the kitchen
  • Rat feces were found at several places in the storage suggesting rodent infestation
  • Expired food items including Real Fruit Orange juice (1L - 08.01.2025), Button Mushrooms (1.6kg - 28.01.2025), Sunflower Organo (600g - Oct 2024), Packed Rai (200g - Dec 2024) were found and discarded
  • Food handlers were found without gloves

Source: TG SAFE, 1 February 2025
