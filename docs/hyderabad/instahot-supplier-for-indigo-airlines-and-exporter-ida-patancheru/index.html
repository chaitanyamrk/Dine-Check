Instahot (Supplier for Indigo airlines and Exporter)

IDA Patancheru, Hyderabad

Enforcement record — no hygiene score.
Action recorded — reported 12 February 2025 by TG SAFE.
This is not a scored audit and is not ranked against places that have one.

This record is from 12 February 2025. It describes conditions on that day and may not reflect the place today.

Violations recorded

  • Improper storage measured followed like storing on ground, use of rusted racks and no FIFO method
  • Expired food articles including Kasturi Methi (1.25kg), Mustard seeds (0.5kg), Rai Mota (1kg) were found and discarded
  • Nutmeg (1.5kg) were found with labelling violations like no MRP, Batch No. and Date of packing and hence seized
  • Few food articles were found stored on ground below a leaking air conditioner, which may lead to contamination of food articles
  • Few vegetables like tomatoes were found to be spoiled and in rotten condition
  • Live cockroach infestation was observed in the cooking area and grinding unit
  • The flooring was found to be patchy and littered with food waste. Drains were found clogged
  • Dustbins are not maintained. Food waste was found dumped in plastic bags
  • Water analysis is being done non-NABL laboratory and hence not acceptable
  • Latest records of testing of packing material is not available

Source: TG SAFE, 12 February 2025
