NALSAR University Canteen (Sri Sai Ganesh Caterers)

Shamirpet, Hyderabad

Enforcement record — no hygiene score.
Action recorded — reported 4 February 2025 by TG SAFE.
This is not a scored audit and is not ranked against places that have one.

This record is from 4 February 2025. It describes conditions on that day and may not reflect the place today.

Violations recorded

  • FSSAI license obtained on registered address instead of food preparation premises
  • FoSTaC trained supervisor not available
  • Pest control records and Water analysis reports were not available
  • Rat faeces and cockroach infestation found in store room
  • Unlabelled raw rajma (90kgs) found
  • Windows and doors of preparation area, dining area do not have insect proof screens
  • Floor found to be slippery in preparation area
  • Temperature records were not maintained
  • Raw materials were kept on floor without pallets
  • Muskmelon seeds used for gravy curries (20 kg) found to be insect infested. Hence discarded
  • Whole green gram (30kg) found without packing date, expiry date and batch no

Source: TG SAFE, 4 February 2025
