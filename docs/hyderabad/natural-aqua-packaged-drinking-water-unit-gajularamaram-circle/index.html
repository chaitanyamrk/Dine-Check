Natural Aqua (Packaged Drinking Water Unit)

Rodamestry Nagar, Gajularamaram Circle, Hyderabad

64/100
Needs work · under 70 band
Inspected 22 June 2026
101 of 158 marks on that checklist

Good practices observed

  • Properly sloped flooring with no water stagnation
  • Wastewater diverted into a rainwater harvesting pit
  • Walls and ceilings found free from dust and cobwebs

Problems recorded

  • Food handlers were not using headcaps, gloves, or aprons
  • Bottling unit lacked proper barriers/mesh to prevent pest and dust entry
  • Pest control, medical fitness, and cleaning schedule records were not available
  • NOC for groundwater extraction was not obtained
  • Water analysis report was incomplete, with no microbial analysis
  • FSSAI license for the transportation vehicle was not available
  • NABL certification for packing materials was not produced
  • Application documents were not properly self-attested

Action taken

Drinking water samples collected for laboratory analysis | FBO instructed to rectify all observations and resubmit the application with complete documentation for further processing

Source: the Cyberabad Municipal Corporation food safety team, 22 June 2026
