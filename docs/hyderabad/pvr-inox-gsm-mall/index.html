Good practices observed

  • Dustbins found open at coffee and sandwich counters

  • Packaging testing records were not produced during inspection

Action taken

Improvement Notice to be issued | Instructions given to maintain all required records and supporting documents | Directions issued to reduce shelf life of prepared ingredients as per food safety requirements | Food handlers instructed to follow proper personal hygiene practices
