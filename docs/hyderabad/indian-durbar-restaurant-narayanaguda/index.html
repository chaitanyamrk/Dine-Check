Indian Durbar Restaurant

Narayanaguda, Hyderabad

Enforcement record — no hygiene score.
Action recorded — reported 3 February 2025 by TG SAFE.
This is not a scored audit and is not ranked against places that have one.

This record is from 3 February 2025. It describes conditions on that day and may not reflect the place today.

Violations recorded

  • Food handlers were found without aprons in kitchen area
  • Boiled eggs were found placed near drains without any covering
  • Severe cockroach infestation was observed in the kitchen
  • Food items were found in fridge without preparation and use by dates
  • Rotten tomatoes, green chillies and carrots with fungal infestation were found and discarded
  • Sanitary items were placed along with food items in store room
  • Pest control records and Medical fitness certificates for food handlers were not available
  • Usage of synthetic food colours was observed
  • Expired food items including Masala Powder (3pkts), Cumin powder (3.5kg - Oct 2024), Hazelnut flavored syrup (Sep 2024), Peach flavored syrup (Mar 2024) and Chilli flakes (Mar 2024) were found and discarded

Source: TG SAFE, 3 February 2025
