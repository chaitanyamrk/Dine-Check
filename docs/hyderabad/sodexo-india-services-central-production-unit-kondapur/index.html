Sodexo India Services Central Production unit

Hitex, Kondapur, Hyderabad

Enforcement record — no hygiene score.
Action recorded — reported 2 April 2025 by the Commissioner of Food Safety, Telangana.
This is not a scored audit and is not ranked against places that have one.

This record is from 2 April 2025. It describes conditions on that day and may not reflect the place today.

Violations recorded

  • Flooring was found to be patchy at some places in the kitchen and grinding area
  • Flooring was found to be wet with littering of food waste in the grinding area
  • Paneer packets (16 kg) for which packing dates was not visible were made to be discarded
  • No proper gap is left between the wall and storage racks to ensure proper cleaning
  • Blood from packed raw meat was found to be dripping in the chiller room
  • Food articles kept in plastic trays stored on floor in cold storage
  • Food waste was found to be accumulated at several places in the kitchen drains
  • Few food articles were kept uncovered in the kitchen below knee height
  • Housefly infestation was observed at the raw material reception area

Source: the Commissioner of Food Safety, Telangana, 2 April 2025
