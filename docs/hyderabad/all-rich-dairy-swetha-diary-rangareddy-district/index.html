All Rich Dairy (Swetha Diary)

Pasumamula(V), Abdullapurmet(M), Hyderabad · also listed as All Rich Dairy

Enforcement record — no hygiene score.
Action recorded — reported 30 December 2024 by TG SAFE.
This is not a scored audit and is not ranked against places that have one.

This record is from 30 December 2024. It describes conditions on that day and may not reflect the place today.

Violations recorded

  • FSSAI License is not displayed at a prominent location and few categories of milk products are not included in the license
  • No proper hygiene and sanitation within the dairy processing unit
  • Rusted machinery found being used. Rusted walls and roof identified in the cold storage chambers and rust seepage is observed where final products of milk and curd are stored
  • Harmful chemicals such as TGV 999 caustic soda (850kg) found at the location and suspected to be used for adulteration
  • No proper storage of raw materials is done. Scrap material found being stored along with final products
  • Multiple label defects identified on the final products including cross-branding and non-declaration of additives like artificial flavors
  • Chemicals and disinfectants found stored in close vicinity of final milk products such as Paneer and Flavoured milks in the same refrigerator
  • Ghee (280kg) seized on suspicion of undeclared additives
  • No water analysis reports and medical test reports of food handlers
  • Non-FSSAI lactic acid cultures found being used in curd making
  • No internal and external audit records available. No documentation such as Quality control records, Waste management, Sanitation and maintenance, Complaint and recall records, etc are being maintained

Source: TG SAFE, 30 December 2024
