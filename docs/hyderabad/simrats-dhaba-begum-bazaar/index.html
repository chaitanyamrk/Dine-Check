Begum Bazaar, Hyderabad

Enforcement record — no hygiene score.
Action initiated under the FSS Act, 2006 — reported 26 July 2024 by TG SAFE.
This is not a scored audit and is not ranked against places that have one.

This record is from 26 July 2024. It describes conditions on that day and may not reflect the place today.

Violations recorded

  • The FBO is found operating with FSSAI Registration Certificate instead of State License even though the business comes under license category
  • Food handlers were found without any hairnets, gloves and aprons
  • Premises was not fitted with insect proof screen avoid entry of pests and doors not closed fitted
  • Dustbins were found open without any lids
  • Exhaust fan and surrounding areas found greasy and untidy
  • Snack Seasoning bottles (2.4kg) were found without proper labelling and hence discarded
  • Medical fitness certificates for food handlers and Pest Control Records for the premises are not available with FBO

Action taken

Action initiated under the FSS Act, 2006
