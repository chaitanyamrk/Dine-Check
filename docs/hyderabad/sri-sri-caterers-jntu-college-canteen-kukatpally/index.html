Sri Sri Caterers (JNTU College Canteen)

JNTU Campus, Kukatpally, Hyderabad

Enforcement record — no hygiene score.
Action recorded — reported 26 June 2024 by TG SAFE.
This is not a scored audit and is not ranked against places that have one.

This record is from 26 June 2024. It describes conditions on that day and may not reflect the place today.

Violations recorded

  • The FBO is having improper FSSAI licence
  • Food handlers were found without wearing headgear, gloves and aprons
  • Semi-prepared food articles and cut vegetables were not covered and labelled properly
  • The kitchen premises was found in unhygienic conditions with food waste thrown directly on the floor
  • The kitchen premises is not fitted with insect proof screen and doors not close fitted to avoid entry of pests
  • Medical fitness certificates and pest control records were not available
  • Rice flour (3.5kg) was found expired (31.05.2024) and hence discarded on the spot

Source: TG SAFE, 26 June 2024
