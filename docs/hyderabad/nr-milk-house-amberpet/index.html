NR Milk House

Rahat Nagar, Amberpet, Hyderabad

Enforcement record — no hygiene score.
Action initiated under the FSS Act, 2006 — reported 20 December 2024 by TG SAFE.
This is not a scored audit and is not ranked against places that have one.

This record is from 20 December 2024. It describes conditions on that day and may not reflect the place today.

Violations recorded

  • Internal structures are made up of iron sheets which were found to be rusty
  • Flooring was found to be patchy, uneven with stagnation of water and was also found to be emanating foul smell
  • One toilet provided which opens directly into the manufacturing premises and may lead to contamination
  • Manufacturing and storage is being done in an unhygienic environment
  • Pest control records are not available and presence of pests like house flies, mosquitoes and cockroaches has been noticed
  • Waste bags, wooden logs, plastic containers etc were found to be dumped and disposed on the floor
  • Food handlers were found without aprons, headgear, shoe cover etc and spitting was observed at some places in the unit
  • Fostac Certificates and Employee health records were not available

Action taken

Action initiated under the FSS Act, 2006

Source: TG SAFE, 20 December 2024
