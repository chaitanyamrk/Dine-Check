Narayana Educational Society

Unspecified, Hyderabad · also listed as Central Kitchen of Narayana Educational Society

Enforcement record — no hygiene score.
Action initiated under the FSS Act, 2006 — reported 10 March 2025 by TG SAFE.
This is not a scored audit and is not ranked against places that have one.

This record is from 10 March 2025. It describes conditions on that day and may not reflect the place today.

Violations recorded

  • Water analysis report does not include testing for microbial contaminants
  • Multiple violations noticed pertaining to the license category availed by the FBO. Instructed to apply for modification as per the rules
  • 9 vehicles are being used for transportation of food but license not obtained with transport category
  • Flooring was found to be patchy and stagnant water. Few wall tiles found to be broken
  • Raw inlet water tank for R.O. plant was found to be unclean
  • Rat feces were observed in the vegetable cutting area
  • Few vegetables like potatoes, tomatoes etc. were found in spoilt condition and hence discarded
  • Rusty iron knives found being used for vegetable cutting
  • Housefly presence was observed at several places in the kitchen which may cause contamination
  • Several food articles found to be stored on ground instead of pallets. Food and non-food articles were stored together
  • Cobwebs were observed at several places in the storage which suggests lack of proper cleaning
  • Doors are not close fitted to prevent entry of pests

Action taken

Action initiated under the FSS Act, 2006

Source: TG SAFE, 10 March 2025
