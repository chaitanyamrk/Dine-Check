KS Bakers

Chintal, Hyderabad

96/100
Excellent · 90–100 band
Inspected 10 July 2026
105 of 110 marks on that checklist

Good practices observed

  • Ready-to-sell and raw food items were properly covered and labelled with preparation and use-by dates
  • Food handlers were wearing uniforms and hairnets
  • Vegetarian and non-vegetarian food items stored separately with proper labelling
  • Dustbins fitted with automatic closing lids
  • Medical fitness certificates, pest control records, water testing reports, and cleaning schedule records were available
  • FIFO and FEFO practices followed for stored food items
  • FSSAI Licence and Trade Licence displayed at the premises

Problems recorded

  • FSSAI licence for transportation vehicles was not available during inspection
  • Food-grade certificates for packaging materials and internal audit reports were not maintained at the premises

Action taken

The Food Business Operator (FBO) has been instructed to rectify the identified deficiencies

Source: the Cyberabad Municipal Corporation food safety team, 10 July 2026
