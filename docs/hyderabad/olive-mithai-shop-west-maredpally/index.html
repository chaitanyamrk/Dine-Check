Olive Mithai Shop

West Maredpally, Hyderabad

Enforcement record — no hygiene score.
Action recorded — reported 18 September 2024 by TG SAFE.
This is not a scored audit and is not ranked against places that have one.

This record is from 18 September 2024. It describes conditions on that day and may not reflect the place today.

Good practices observed

  • FSSAI License true copy was displayed at the billing counter
  • Food handlers were found wearing haircaps and uniforms

Violations recorded

  • Medical Fitness Certificates for food handlers and Pest Control Records for the premises not available with the FBO
  • Packed food articles kept on display for sale like Namkeen, Mixture, Chakodi, etc were not properly labelled i.e., FSSAI License Number, Logo and Manufacturer details were not printed on them
  • Few sweets which are packed and sold at the premises were found without following proper labelling regulations

Source: TG SAFE, 18 September 2024
