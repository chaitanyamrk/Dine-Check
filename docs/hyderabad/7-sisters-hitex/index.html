Near NAC Building, Hitex, Hyderabad

73/100
Average · 70–79 band
Inspected 20 June 2026
71 of 98 marks on that checklist

Good practices observed

  • Food handlers were using hairnets, gloves, and aprons
  • Medical fitness, pest control, and water testing records were available
  • Proper segregation of veg and non-veg food items
  • No expired products found in the store room
  • No major pest infestation observed

Problems recorded

  • Prepared food items were not being discarded within the prescribed use-by period
  • Meat items were not properly labelled
  • Food items were found kept open near drain areas without adequate covering
  • Broken tiles and slippery flooring observed in the kitchen
  • Hot water sanitation practices were not being followed
  • Lizard droppings found in the store room, indicating inadequate shelf cleaning

Action taken

Improvement Notice is being issued | Instructions given to strengthen food storage, labelling, sanitation, and housekeeping practices

Source: the Cyberabad Municipal Corporation food safety team, 20 June 2026
