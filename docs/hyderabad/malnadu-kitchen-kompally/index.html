Malnadu Kitchen

Kompally, Hyderabad

Enforcement record — no hygiene score.
Action recorded — reported 30 December 2024 by TG SAFE.
This is not a scored audit and is not ranked against places that have one.

This record is from 30 December 2024. It describes conditions on that day and may not reflect the place today.

Violations recorded

  • FSSAI license not displayed at the premises
  • Water analysis reports, Pest Control records, Fostac certificates and Health Records of employees were not provided
  • Raw meet like chicken, mutton etc were found to be dumped in the refrigerator in unhygienic manner which can cause contamination
  • Prepared food articles like Mutton Curry & Fried Chicken Wings were stored in the cold room without proper labelling, which were discarded
  • Synthetic food colors were found and discarded on suspicion of use in prepared food articles
  • Rodent excreta was found in several places in the storage
  • Refrigerators were not maintained in hygienic condition
  • Flooring was found to be patchy and littering of food waste at some places
  • Walls were found to be oily and tiles were found to be broken at some places
  • Dust bins were found in open condition
  • Paint brush found being used to apply butter
  • Raw and semi-cooked food, veg and non-veg food articles were found stored together

Source: TG SAFE, 30 December 2024
