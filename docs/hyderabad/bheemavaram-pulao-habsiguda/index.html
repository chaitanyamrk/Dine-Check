Violations recorded

  • FBO operating the business with registration instead of state license
  • FSSAI license true copy not displayed at the premises
  • Pest control records and Medical fitness certificates were not available with FBO
  • Doors and windows were found open without insect proof screens
  • Semi prepared and raw food articles stored inside the refrigerator were not covered and not labelled
  • Fungus infested carrots were found inside the refrigerator. Hence discarded
  • Veg and non veg food articles stored in the same refrigerator
  • Cockroach infestation observed in the storage racks in the kitchen
  • Food handlers found without wearing head caps, gloves and aprons
  • ⁠Dust bins found open in the kitchen
  • Synthetic food colours found in the kitchen with suspected use in the prepared foods. Hence discarded

Source: TG SAFE, 3 September 2024
