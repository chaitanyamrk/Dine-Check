Know before you eat.

Official food-safety records for restaurants, cafés and stores across India — inspection scores, enforcement findings and FSSAI hygiene ratings, straight from the source.

What this covers

Read scores as percentages. Inspection checklists differ by business type, so total marks vary between establishments. Only the percentage is comparable.

A score is a snapshot, not a standing verdict — it reflects one visit on one day, and conditions change. Places with no recent inspection simply have not been visited, which is not a mark against them. Distances are measured to the centre of a locality, so treat them as approximate.
